[Notes]
- WAEnhancerX now requires a rooted device for reliable compatibility and to protect the module's integrity.
- Rootless setups such as LSPatch or Shizuku are not supported. They may behave unpredictably and increase the risk of WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business account restrictions, so please do not use them with WAEnhancerX.
